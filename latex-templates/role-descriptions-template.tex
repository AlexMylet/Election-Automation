\section*{What do the roles do?}
The JCR officers are responsible for overseeing and running the JCR.
They are all part of the JCR General Committee,
and have a responsibility to make sure that the
decisions made in JCR meetings are carried out.
Each officer has a different role: the following descriptions are
taken from Standing Order Table Two of the JCR Constitution.
