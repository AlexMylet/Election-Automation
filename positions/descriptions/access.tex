Shall work with the College Schools Liaison Officer and Tutor for Admissions to coordinate and promote the Student Ambassador Scheme (SAS) and other initiatives for access to the College. 
Must undertake SAS training at the first available opportunity, if not already an Ambassador. 
Shall, with the assistance of the General Committee when required, maintain and update the Alternative Prospectus for distribution at college open days and/or on the JCR website.
Shall be present for the interview period in December following their term of office. 
Will assist with open days wherever possible, and shall organise the annual school visit road trip, normally in 9th week Trinity term.
Shall advertise all available College and university scholarships to the JCR and represent the JCR when Reach Scholarships are being decided by the university.
Should liaise with the College web officer to help organise photo shoots, and act as a point of contact between the College Web Officer and the JCR. 
Should attend relevant Oxford SU meetings.
