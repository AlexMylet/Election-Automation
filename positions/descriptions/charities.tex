Shall be responsible for liaising with and promoting external charities in the Oxford area and beyond.
Shall work with the treasurer to manage the Charities Budget.
Shall organise at least one fundraising event each term.
Shall organise a charity swap event in Trinity Term, at which students may donate unwanted items for reuse by other students, with any remaining items donated to charity.
Advertise and administer the Charities Polls, including the nominations poll and the final poll, in cooperation with the Treasurer.
