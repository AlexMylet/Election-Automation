Shall organise and direct efforts to address issues of inequality within college, liaising with the College Equalities Officer.
Shall organise and chair the Equality and Diversity Sub-Committee at least once per term. 
Must represent and support the views of all E\&D groups within the JCR, especially on any College or university committees on which this Officer sits.
Must attend the termly Student Equality Forum, also encouraging attendance of the E\&D Reps.
Must attend the termly Equality Committee, designating an E\&D Rep to attend in their place when unable to. 
Shall coordinate and assist with the organisation of intersectional events each term with the E\&D Reps. They should also support other E\&D Officers in their work where necessary.
Shall, where there is a vacancy for Women's Officer or any of the E\&D Reps:\begin{enumerate}
endeavour to fulfil the duties of these Officers as far as reasonably practicable;
in conjunction with the President, appoint other Officers or JCR members to vacant E\&D positions where necessary and with discretion, being sensitive to the needs of that E\&D group when doing so. The JCR must be updated when such appointments are made.\end{enumerate}
Must attend the termly Student Equality Forum and run an Equalities Week in Hilary term.
Should attend relevant Oxford SU meetings and encourage involvement with campaigns surrounding equality and diversity around Oxford.
Should oversee and/or organise at least one E\&D social per term in addition to the formal dinners put on by college.
