Shall organise:\begin{enumerate}
at least three Bops per term, except in Trinity term where at least two shall be held before the College “Events ban”;
supervision for the duration of all internally held Bops in the form of `Bop Angels', enlisting the help of the JCR General Committee where slots are unfilled; 
at least one non-Bop event per term.\end{enumerate}
Shall sit on the Freshers' Week Sub-Committee, responsible for:\begin{enumerate}
the negotiation for, selling and distribution of club tickets for at least 2 nights during the week;
providing assistance for any further Entz events, as requested by the Vice President.\end{enumerate}
Must keep in good order the JCR cupboard in the Kendrew Events Room, carefully maintaining any JCR owned equipment. May use discretion as to the lending of any equipment to JCR members.
Shall lead an annual clear-out of the Kendrew Events Room cupboard, assisted by the JCR General Committee.
Set up and tidy up all events, assisted by the JCR General Committee when required.
Shall endeavour to have at least one social partnered with other JCRs each term, including bops and formal swaps.
