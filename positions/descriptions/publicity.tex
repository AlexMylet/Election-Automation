Shall manage the JCR Instagram Account, ensuring that content appropriate to both current and prospective students is updated regularly. 
Shall promote the JCR's activities to a wide range of stakeholders, including but not limited to prospective students and alumni. 
Shall co-ordinate with other JCR officers to advertise JCR elections, meetings, events and post introductions to officers. 
Work with JCR Sub-Committees, including but not exclusive to the Freshers' Week Committee, Arts Week Committee and the Ball Committee to promote their activities. 
Shall liaise and collaborate with external organisations and other colleges' JCRs as necessary. 
Should manage all other social media accounts held by the JCR ensuring appropriate content provision, with the exception of the JCR Facebook group and the JCR website.
Shall be responsible for and shall fill in for the Social Media Reps.
