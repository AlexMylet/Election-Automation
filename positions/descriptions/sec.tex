Shall maintain the JCR's Constitution and assist the President in ensuring that it is adhered to, as well as checking that the requirements set out in §3 have been fulfilled. This includes reading Charity Commission, NUS and Oxford SU guidance on political campaigning.
Shall minute JCR General Meetings, which should be made available for all members to see via an online link or a projector display during the meeting and uploaded to the JCR Website following the meeting. 
Shall minute JCR General Committee meetings and should make the minutes of all Committee and Subcommittee meetings available to members upon request.
Must keep a record of all individuals who have opted-out of JCR membership.
Shall store all of the JCR's files and records across their tenure, creating a file (or electronic folder) for the year containing these, and, with the President, shall be responsible for managing the JCR SharePoint, including overseeing the uploading of documents, and the removal and addition of JCR Officers. These files are to include all minutes of College Committees the JCR has representation on.
Shall collect and maintain, prior to an election for an Officer role, the associated handover document from the previous Officer.
Must ensure that all Trustees of the JCR have signed the necessary documentation (Appendix VI), keeping a record of these for the duration of their tenure.
Shall ensure the JCR Office is maintained in good order, handing relevant folders to the College Archivist when deemed appropriate.
Shall maintain the Appendices to this Constitution, ensuring their continual accuracy.
Must keep JCR Standing Policy up to date and accessible on the JCR Website, removing expired items and proposing motions to remove completed items as appropriate.
Should maintain a record of the names and contact details of all JCR Officers, Reps, and Sports Captains, as well as College staff positions. This will be transferred to the newly elected Secretary in Hilary Term.
Shall be responsible for the PRAT Rep(s).
